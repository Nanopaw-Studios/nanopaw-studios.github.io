# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

**Critter Cab Company** is developed by **Nanopaw** ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you play Critter Cab Company, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

By playing Critter Cab Company, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## What We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate Critter Cab Company and provide its online features. This may include:

* Your chosen display name or player name
* A device- or platform-based identifier used to create and recognise your game account
* Game progression and save data
* Player customisation preferences
* Owned or unlocked in-game content
* Game statistics and other data required to provide game features
* Information necessary to establish multiplayer connections

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the game, and we do not collect information beyond what is reasonably necessary to operate and improve Critter Cab Company.

---

## Third-Party Services

Critter Cab Company relies on trusted third-party services to provide its online features.

### Microsoft PlayFab

PlayFab is used for account management and online game data, which may include:

* Account creation and identification
* Cloud-saved game progress
* Player data and statistics
* Inventory or owned content
* Other online game data required by Critter Cab Company

PlayFab may process information associated with your game account in order to provide these services.

[PlayFab Privacy Policy](https://playfab.com/privacy/) · [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacy)

### Photon Engine

Photon is used to provide Critter Cab Company's real-time multiplayer networking.

Photon may process network information, such as IP addresses, in order to establish and maintain connections between players and its networking infrastructure.

Critter Cab Company does not store or intentionally process players' IP addresses itself.

[Photon Privacy Policy](https://www.photonengine.com/privacy-policy)

---

## Friend-Only Multiplayer

Critter Cab Company uses a friend-only multiplayer system.

Multiplayer sessions are intended to be joined by people invited by or connected to the host/player. The game does not provide a public matchmaking system where players are randomly matched with the general player population.

Photon may nevertheless process the network information necessary to establish multiplayer connections between participants in a session.

---

## Voice Chat

If voice chat is enabled in Critter Cab Company, voice communications are transmitted in real time through the game's networking service to other players in your multiplayer session.

We do not record, monitor, or store voice communications.

---

## Analytics & Advertising

Critter Cab Company does not use third-party advertising services and does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

Unless otherwise stated in-game or in a future update to this policy, we do not use third-party analytics services to build advertising profiles or track players across unrelated services.

We use the information collected through Critter Cab Company to:

* Create and identify your game account
* Save and restore your game progress
* Provide online and multiplayer functionality
* Connect you with friends and other members of your multiplayer session
* Deliver owned or unlocked game content
* Maintain and improve the stability of the game and its online services
* Prevent abuse, cheating, or other activity that could negatively affect the game or its players
